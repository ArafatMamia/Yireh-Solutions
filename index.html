03 About

Technology that
holds up.

YIREH Solutions is an IT solutions and technology services company. We help organisations build infrastructure they can rely on, secure their premises and data, and use technology to work more efficiently.

Our work covers IT infrastructure, networking, security systems, business management software, telecommunications and software development — usually in combination, because these systems only work well when they are designed together.

We work with businesses, organisations and institutions: from a single office that needs reliable Wi-Fi and cameras, to a multi-site operation running ERP, VoIP and managed support.

Vision

To become a trusted technology partner providing integrated IT solutions and infrastructure services to businesses in Ethiopia and beyond.

Mission

To deliver reliable, practical and affordable technology solutions that help businesses operate securely, efficiently and intelligently.
